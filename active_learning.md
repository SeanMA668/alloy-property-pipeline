## How well the models predict (leave-one-out)

| Target | Spearman ρ | Mean abs. error (°C) | Error of always guessing the mean (°C) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Split temperature | 0.94 | 22 | 122 |
| Solidus | 0.98 | 14 | 139 |

The split model's raw uncertainty was 1.5× too narrow on held-out alloys, so all ± values and probabilities below are widened by that factor.

For comparison, the cross-family ML model's P(BCC) reaches |ρ| = 0.55 on the split temperature for the same alloys (`calphad_vs_ml.md`). With so few points these errors are rough; they will tighten as rounds are added.
