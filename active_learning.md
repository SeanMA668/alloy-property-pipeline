# Active learning, round 2

Models fitted on 14 Thermo-Calc results (`data/calphad/calphad_vs_ml.csv`). Generated by `active_learning.py`.

## How well the models predict (leave-one-out)

| Target | Spearman ρ | Mean abs. error (°C) | Error of always guessing the mean (°C) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Split temperature | 0.82 | 61 | 115 |
| Solidus | 0.92 | 40 | 117 |

The split model's raw uncertainty was 2.2× too narrow on held-out alloys, so all ± values and probabilities below are widened by that factor.

For comparison, the cross-family ML model's P(BCC) reaches |ρ| = 0.43 on the split temperature for the same alloys (`calphad_vs_ml.md`). With so few points these errors are rough; they will tighten as rounds are added.
